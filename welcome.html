Make your copy of Rostas

First, use a browser with only your church's Google account in it. Your copy of Rostas belongs to whichever Google account makes it, and Google won't open Rostas in a browser signed in to two accounts at once (you'd see “Sorry, unable to open the file”). Signing out of your other accounts isn't always enough. The reliable way, in Chrome: click your profile picture (top right) → Add → sign in with the church account only. Do the rest of set-up in that new window.

Then, in that window, click the button, and click Make a copy when Google asks. Your own private Rostas sheet opens in a new tab. From there, follow the guide.

Make your copy of Rostas →
No video? Read the steps instead

These are the same steps the clips show, starting once your copy has opened.

  1. Open the script editor In your new sheet, open the Extensions menu and choose Apps Script. It opens in another tab. You won't need to touch any code.
  2. Deploy Rostas At the top right, click Deploy, then New deployment. Click the cog beside “Select type” and choose Web app. Set “Execute as” to Me and “Who has access” to Anyone, then click Deploy.
  3. Get past Google's warning Click the blue Authorise access button and choose your Google account. Google then says it “hasn't verified this app”. That is normal for a new copy of Rostas, and safe to click through. Click the small Advanced link at the bottom left. A new line appears underneath. Click Go to… (unsafe).
  4. Choose what to share A sign-in screen appears. Click Continue. The next screen lists what your copy of Rostas needs. Tick Select all at the top of the list, scroll to the bottom and click Continue. If you skip Select all, parts of Rostas will not work later.
  5. Copy your Web app URL The deployment window now shows a Deployment ID and, below it, a Web app URL. You need the Web app URL. Click the small Copy button beside it, not your browser's address bar. Save the link somewhere safe as well.
  6. Open Rostas Open a new browser tab, paste the link and press Enter. The Rostas Setup Wizard starts. Bookmark this page now (Ctrl+D, or Cmd+D on a Mac), because you will open it often. If Google says “Sorry, unable to open the file”, your browser is signed in to more than one Google account. Do set-up in a Chrome profile with only the church account (see step 2 above). Lost the link? Open your sheet, then Extensions → Apps Script → Deploy → Manage deployments, and copy the Web app URL again.
  7. Enter your licence key The wizard's first step asks for it. Paste in the key from the top of this page.
  8. Put Rostas on your desktop In your Church Chrome window, with Rostas open, click the three dots (top right) → Cast, save and share → Install page as app → Install. A Rostas icon appears on your desktop (on a Mac, in Launchpad and the Dock). From now on, open Rostas from that icon. The coordinator guide has the details: Put Rostas on your desktop.

What comes next. The Setup Wizard takes you through your organisation's details and a test text. The coordinator guide explains each step: The Setup Wizard.

Stuck on any step? Email support@rostas.app and we'll help you through it.
