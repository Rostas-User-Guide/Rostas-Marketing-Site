Welcome to the Rostas beta

Thank you for helping us test Rostas. Your church's subscription is free for the whole beta.

  • No card needed to start.
  • 20 free texts to get you going. After that, text credits are 12.5c a text, bought when you need them.
  • We'll give you at least 30 days' notice before the beta ends. You won't be charged for Rostas unless you then choose to subscribe.

Next you'll see a Stripe page showing NZ$0.00. Enter the email address your church will use for Rostas and click Subscribe. Your licence key and set-up steps come straight after.

Questions? Email support@rostas.app. By signing up you agree to our Terms and Privacy Policy.
