1. Who we are

Rostas is volunteer rostering software made in New Zealand by Guy Pilkinton, trading as Rostas, 20 Battiscombe Terrace, Waitara 4320, New Zealand. In this policy "we" and "us" mean Rostas.

Questions or requests about privacy: info@rostas.app.

2. The short version

  • Your volunteer list and rosters live in your own Google account, in a spreadsheet you own. We do not hold a copy.
  • Some information passes through our systems so that texts, app notifications and short links work. Section 5 lists exactly what.
  • We do not sell personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.
  • We follow the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020. For people in Australia we also follow the Australian Privacy Principles where they apply to us.

3. Two kinds of information

Information about you as our customer or website visitor. We decide how this is used, and we are responsible for it.

Information about your volunteers. Your organisation collects this and decides how it is used. Your organisation is responsible for it under privacy law, including telling volunteers what is collected and why. We only handle it on your organisation's behalf, to provide the service.

5. Information about volunteers

Held in your Google account, not by us: names, mobile numbers, email addresses, roles and role preferences, availability, household and buddy links, how often each person serves, notes, rosters, message history, and a record of which devices have the Rostas app.

Emails to volunteers are sent from your own Google account.

Optional features.

  • Infoodle. If your organisation connects Infoodle, names, mobile numbers and email addresses are copied from Infoodle into your spreadsheet.
  • AI roster import. Rostas includes an optional tool for importing an old roster. It sends the roster you paste in, together with your volunteer names and role names, to OpenAI so the names can be matched. It is switched off unless your organisation adds its own OpenAI key, and it asks for confirmation each time before anything is sent.

6. The Rostas app

The app is optional. It installs from a personal link, with no password and no app store account.

  • On the phone, the app stores the volunteer's name, the organisation's name, the details that keep them signed in, and a history of notifications received.
  • The app does not collect location, contacts, photos or anything else from the phone.
  • Notifications are delivered by the phone maker's notification service (Google for Android and Chrome, Apple for iPhone and iPad). The content is encrypted in transit so those services cannot read it.
  • A volunteer can turn notifications off, or remove the app, from the app's Settings at any time.

8. Information held outside New Zealand and Australia

Most of the companies above store information outside New Zealand and Australia. We choose providers that protect information to a standard comparable to New Zealand law, and we remain responsible for information they hold for us.

9. How long we keep information

  • Customer and licence records: for as long as you have a subscription, then for up to seven years where tax and accounting law requires it.
  • App notification details and short links: as in section 5.
  • Volunteer information in your spreadsheet: for as long as your organisation keeps it. It is yours to delete.

10. Security

Connections to our systems are encrypted. Access to our licence system, payment records and provider accounts is limited to the owner of Rostas. Text-carrier and payment credentials are never placed in your copy of Rostas.

Each coordinator link and volunteer link works like a key. Anyone who has the link can use it, so links should not be forwarded or posted. A coordinator can issue a volunteer a new link at any time, which cancels the old one.

11. Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of the personal information we hold about you, and ask us to correct it. Email info@rostas.app. We will answer within 20 working days.

If you are not satisfied with our answer you can complain to:

  • New Zealand: Office of the Privacy Commissioner, privacy.org.nz
  • Australia: Office of the Australian Information Commissioner, oaic.gov.au

12. If you are a volunteer

Your organisation holds your information, so ask your roster coordinator first if you want to see it, correct it or have it removed. If you contact us we will pass your request to your organisation and help it respond.

Every Rostas email and text includes a link where you can pause or stop messages yourself.

13. If something goes wrong

If there is a privacy breach involving our systems that is likely to cause serious harm, we will tell the affected organisations and the Privacy Commissioner as soon as we can, and the Australian Information Commissioner where Australian law requires it.

14. Changes

We will post changes on this page and change the date at the top. If a change is significant we will email the contact for each subscription first.
